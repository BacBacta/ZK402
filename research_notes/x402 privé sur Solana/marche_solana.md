# x402 market on Solana and demand for privacy (as of 25 Sept 2026)

## Q1. x402 traction on Solana vs Base (transactions, volume, buyers and sellers, facilitators, share)

### Takeaway
By September 2026 Solana leads x402 on recent activity. Artemis data, which the official Solana account cited on 22 Sept 2026, puts Solana at about 76% of transactions over 4 weeks: 23.2M, against 3.39M on the next chain. Base still leads, or recently led, on **cumulative, all-time** volume, because of the Base-heavy launch surge in Oct–Nov 2025. The "65% vs Base 58%" conflict comes mostly from different metrics (YTD-2026 volume vs cumulative since inception) and different dates. Organic, non-wash activity is much smaller than the headline numbers.

### Cited Findings
**Recent figures (Aug–Sept 2026)**
- For the 4 weeks ending 22 Sept 2026, Solana handled 23.2M x402 transactions, about 76% of all x402 activity. The second-largest network handled 3.39M. The source is Artemis data, cited by the official Solana account on 22 Sept 2026. — [Solana Compass, 22 Sept 2026](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)
- Same article: the all-chain 30-day total was 75.41M transactions and $24.24M in payment volume. In one August week, Solana settled $3.3M in USDC. — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)
  - CONFLICT/UNCERTAINTY: Crypto Briefing (15 Sept 2026) says the $24.24M 30-day figure is **Solana's** volume, not the all-chain total. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/). Also, 23.2M Solana transactions out of 75.41M total is about 31%, not 76%, so the 4-week and 30-day windows (or the trackers behind them) probably differ. Treat both numbers carefully.
- In mid-August 2026, Solana had about 70% of monthly x402 volume across all chains and more than 35M cumulative x402 transactions. On 25 Aug 2026 it passed Base in **daily** x402 transaction count for the first time in six months. — [Crypto Briefing, 25 Aug 2026](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-surpasses-base-x402-transactions/); [Crypto Briefing, 15 Sept 2026](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/)
- A cited driver: on 21 Aug 2026 Ramp announced x402 AI-agent wallet capabilities on Solana, aimed at more than 70,000 businesses. Nearly all x402 payments settle in USDC. — [Crypto Briefing, 25 Aug 2026](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-surpasses-base-x402-transactions/)
- Solana Foundation's own x402 page (accessed Sept 2026) claims 37M+ transactions on Solana, 20K+ buyers and sellers, and "70% monthly volume on Solana". — [solana.com/x402](https://solana.com/x402)
- Another report says Solana processed more than 90% of x402 transactions in one recent week. The exact date is not verified. — [Coin-Turk](https://en.coin-turk.com/solana-overtakes-base-with-90yuzde-share-in-x402-transaction-volume-and-count/) (secondary source, unverified)
- Tracker estimates of cumulative all-chain x402 transactions range from 120M to 187M. — [Crypto Briefing, 15 Sept 2026](https://cryptobriefing.com/solana-x402-market-share-dominance/)

**Earlier figures (volatility)**

**Facilitators**
- On Solana (about Feb 2026), Dexter had 68.7% of transactions and 75% of volume, and PayAI had 30.5% of transactions and 14.7% of volume. — [SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/solana-commands-49-of-x402-market-share-as-the-race-for-micropayment-dominance-intensifies)
- Solana Foundation's x402 page lists Corbits, PayAI and the CDP (Coinbase) wallet. Its tooling includes Faremeter (an open-source framework), the x402Secure SDK, an MCP server, and x402scan as the explorer. — [solana.com/x402](https://solana.com/x402)
- Coinbase shipped an upgraded x402 facilitator on 18 Sept 2026. Other named integrations are AWS CloudFront (pay-per-AI-crawler), Messari and Nansen. — [Solana Compass](https://solanacompass.com/news/solana-processes-76-of-all-x402-ai-agent-transactions-232-million-in-four-weeks)
- The web3trackers leaderboard, which is Base-label-centric (14 Apr 2026), shows Daydreams (Base) at 8.1M+ transactions, Coinbase (3 entities, Base and Solana) at 7.6M+, x402rs at 411K+ and Canza at 12K+. It does not break out Dexter or PayAI. — [web3trackers](https://www.web3trackers.com/x402-dashboard)

**Resolving the 65% vs 58% conflict**
- Linux Foundation, April 2026: Solana drove "nearly 65% of x402 transaction volume **this year**", i.e. year-to-date 2026. — [Linux Foundation press release](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol)
- web3trackers (last updated 14 Apr 2026): Base 58% ($21.5M), Solana 38% ($16.4M), other chains 4% ($3.1M). This is **cumulative USDC volume since launch**. Counts come from BaseScan x402 labels and Solana program indexers, and volume comes from x402station. Average payment size is $0.05. — [web3trackers](https://www.web3trackers.com/x402-dashboard)
- The web3trackers cumulative dollar figures ($41M, $21.5M, $16.4M) exactly match SolanaFloor's Feb 2026 figures, so the dashboard may not have been refreshed with new volume. — [SolanaFloor](https://solanafloor.com/news/solana-commands-49-of-x402-market-share-as-the-race-for-micropayment-dominance-intensifies)

### Inferences
- The two claims do not contradict each other. Base built its cumulative lead during the Oct–Dec 2025 launch and token-mint surge, much of it non-organic. Solana dominated 2026 YTD flows, and that is where the Linux Foundation's 65% comes from. The metric (cumulative vs period, count vs USD, which facilitators are indexed) and the date explain most of the gap.
- Absolute USD volume is small: tens of millions per month at best, with an average payment of about $0.05. Transaction counts overstate economic activity.
- Share swings widely (49.7%–90%) from week to week. That points to concentration in a few facilitators or bots rather than a broad organic base.

### Gaps
- I did not pull x402scan, Dune or Artemis dashboards directly (not fetched in this pass). All figures are secondary reports of those sources.
- No reliable Sept 2026 count of unique buyers and sellers per chain, and no current per-facilitator split (Dexter, PayAI, Corbits, Coinbase) on Solana.
- No organic vs wash split for Aug–Sept 2026.

## Q2. Solana Foundation and ecosystem stance on agent payments and x402

### Takeaway
The Solana Foundation actively promotes x402. It runs a dedicated site with facilitators and templates, joined the Linux Foundation's x402 Foundation (April 2026), and puts x402 at the centre of its agentic-payments docs. The agent developer stack (SendAI's Solana Agent Kit, ElizaOS, GOAT, Rig) and hackathons give it wide reach.

### Cited Findings
- The Foundation has an x402 hub (solana.com/x402) listing facilitators, Faremeter, x402Secure, MCP server, templates and x402scan. — [solana.com/x402](https://solana.com/x402). It also publishes developer docs for x402 V2 and facilitator verify and settle. — [Solana docs x402](https://solana.com/docs/payments/agentic-payments/x402); [Solana facilitator docs](https://solana.com/docs/tools/x402-facilitator)
- Solana was named as an early adopter when the Linux Foundation launched the x402 Foundation (April 2026, 20+ members including Google, Microsoft and Visa). — [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-is-launching-the-x402-foundation-and-welcoming-the-contribution-of-the-x402-protocol); [Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/363173/coinbase-linux-foundation-launch-x402-foundation)
- Agent frameworks: SendAI's Solana Agent Kit is the reference LLM-to-Solana toolkit. Crossmint GOAT and Rig are also used. ElizaOS has more than 17,600 GitHub stars. — [Alchemy blog, 2026](https://www.alchemy.com/blog/how-to-build-solana-ai-agents-in-2026)
- The SendAI Solana AI Hackathon had 400+ submissions and more than $250K in prizes. This is an earlier event, around early 2025. — [Alchemy](https://www.alchemy.com/blog/how-to-build-solana-ai-agents-in-2026); [hackathon.sendai.fun](https://hackathon.sendai.fun/)
- The winner of the Solana Student Hackathon, Quarry, built an x402-gated data marketplace with a reputation system based on Solana Attestation Service. — [Alchemy](https://www.alchemy.com/blog/how-to-build-solana-ai-agents-in-2026)

### Inferences
- Solana's institutional push for x402 is strong. It is competing directly with Coinbase/Base for the "agent payments settlement layer" narrative.

### Gaps
- No dedicated x402 grant-program amounts found. There are also no dated results for a Solana x402-specific hackathon in 2026.

## Q3. Documented demand for privacy in Solana agent payments and Solana payments

### Takeaway
Privacy is an explicit 2026 priority of Solana leadership and the Foundation: a privacy spectrum, Privacy Hack in Jan 2026, a Privacy report in Mar 2026, and Arcium mainnet. Agent-specific privacy demand exists but is early. Small builder projects (SNAP, Qietr) argue that public x402 payment graphs leak business intelligence, and academic papers flag privacy and PII leakage in x402. No adoption metrics for private agent payments were found.

### Cited Findings
- **Foundation stance**: privacy as a "spectrum". "Developers should choose the privacy level their app needs. Payroll, trading, and governance do not all need the same disclosure model." The Foundation sets out four levels (pseudonymity, anonymity, confidentiality, full privacy through MPC/FHE) and stresses compliance through selective disclosure. Confidential Token Extensions are live on mainnet. — [solana.com/privacy](https://solana.com/privacy); [Solana docs: privacy for financial apps](https://solana.com/docs/finance/privacy)
- **Privacy Hack**: in January 2026 the Foundation ran a privacy hackathon with $100K+ in prizes; Arcium was one of the sponsors. — [solana.com/privacyhack](https://solana.com/privacyhack); [Trojan blog](https://trojan.com/blog/privacy-on-solana)
- **Leadership**: Anatoly Yakovenko ("Toly") argues that public chains can't become mainstream financial rails if sensitive transactions stay publicly visible. The article paraphrases him (no direct quotes) and dates it 14 May 2026. It says the Solana Foundation released a "Privacy on Solana" report in March 2026, and that SOL Strategies bought Darklake Labs (Zyga ZK) for $1.2M in April 2026. — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/solana-co-founder-toly-positions-privacy-as-cryptos-next-moat/)
- **Scale**: Solana settled about $650B in stablecoin volume in Feb 2026 (The Block via Grayscale and Allium, as cited by Startup Fortune). — [Startup Fortune](https://startupfortune.com/solana-co-founder-toly-positions-privacy-as-cryptos-next-moat/)
- **Arcium**: mainnet alpha in Feb 2026, and over 1M confidential computations by early June 2026. Its C-SPL confidential token standard offers encrypted balances plus an auditor key. — [Trojan Medium, Apr 2026](https://medium.com/@trojantrading/privacy-is-finally-coming-to-solana-in-2026-and-the-infrastructure-is-already-live-c773048c8137); [Solana Compass Arcium](https://solanacompass.com/projects/Arcium) (partly from search snippets; verify)
- **PayPal PYUSD**: launched on Solana in May 2024 with the confidential-transfer extension *initialized* so merchants could hide amounts while staying visible to regulators. Reporting at the time noted it was initialized, not yet enabled or used. I found no confirmation of active use in 2026. — [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/paypal-pyusd-stablecoin-confidential-transfers-solana); [PayPal dev blog](https://developer.paypal.com/community/blog/pyusd-solana-token-extensions/)
- **Agent builders' pain (SNAP, 5 June 2026)**: "When every on-chain agent payment is public, an agent's entire financial graph becomes visible… A competitor doesn't need to hack your agent. They just need a block explorer." SNAP uses Groth16 commitment and nullifier pools (SOL and USDC) and claims to be live on mainnet. It gives no usage figures. — [DEV Community](https://dev.to/agentzeny/how-we-built-private-agent-payments-on-solana-with-zero-knowledge-proofs-kmh)
- **Qietr**: unlinkable USDC payments for AI agents over x402, using a Groth16 zk-SNARK. — [qietr.com](https://qietr.com/) (self-description; no traction data)
- **Academic work**:
  - A402 states that x402 "suffers from scalability and privacy limitations, as all interactions are recorded on-chain, compromising business privacy". — [arXiv 2603.01179](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.01179)
  - Another paper shows that x402 payment metadata (resource URL, description, reason) can leak PII to servers and facilitators. — [arXiv 2604.11430](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.11430)
  - Other risk and attack studies: [arXiv 2607.19545](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.19545); [arXiv 2605.11781](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.11781) (contents not fetched)
- Solana's official x402 page does not mention privacy beyond "no accounts or subscriptions". — [solana.com/x402](https://solana.com/x402)

### Inferences
- There is a clear gap: the Foundation pushes x402 and privacy separately, and its official x402 stack ships no privacy option. So "private x402 on Solana" is an unoccupied or early niche; the known players are small (SNAP, Qietr).
- Demand evidence is mostly supply-side (builders, papers, Foundation strategy). There is not yet dated buyer demand such as enterprise RFPs or user surveys.

### Gaps
- No dated statements from Visa or Stripe about privacy needs on Solana were found in this pass. There was no dated enterprise privacy requirement specific to agent payments either.
- No usage figures for SNAP, Qietr or Arcium-based payments.
- The text of the Solana Foundation "Privacy on Solana" report (Mar 2026) was not fetched.

## Q4. Size of the AI-agent ecosystem on Solana (2026)

### Takeaway
Developer activity is large (ElizaOS: 1,350+ contributors, 50K+ agents claimed), but agent-token valuations have collapsed. The AI-agent token category is about $3.2B (Aug 2026), and the ai16z/ELIZAOS token is down more than 99.9% from its peak and faces a class action.

### Cited Findings
- The ELIZAOS (formerly ai16z) token is down more than 99.9% from its peak (it was once worth $2.4B), and a proposed class action alleges insiders controlled the supposedly autonomous fund. By mid-2026, ElizaOS had 5,300+ forks and 1,350+ contributors, and claimed 50,000+ agents deployed. — [Motley Fool, 20 Aug 2026](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/20/an-ai-agent-token-once-worth-24-billion-is-now-wor/)
- The AI-agents token category was worth about $3.2B as of 18 Aug 2026. VIRTUAL was 88% below its Jan 2025 high, with a market cap of about $490M. Virtuals expanded to Solana earlier. — [Motley Fool via search snippet](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/20/an-ai-agent-token-once-worth-24-billion-is-now-wor/); [Criptolog](https://www.criptolog.com/coin/ai-agent-tokens-surge-as-virtuals-protocol-expands-to-solana/)
- ElizaOS has more than 17,600 GitHub stars. — [Alchemy](https://www.alchemy.com/blog/how-to-build-solana-ai-agents-in-2026)
- A new AI-agent platform is expected to lift Solana (late Aug 2026), but the platform's name was not confirmed in this pass. — [Motley Fool, 29 Aug 2026](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/08/29/this-new-ai-agent-platform-could-send-solanas-pric/)

### Inferences
- Speculative interest in agent tokens has faded, but the builder and infrastructure base has held up. Payments infrastructure like x402 is moving the narrative from "agent tokens" to "agent commerce".
